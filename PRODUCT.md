# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

People who own and drive a car and want one place for its service history and plain-language answers when a warning light or fault code appears. Inferred (not yet confirmed by the owner): web buyers of Pro are mostly Android users worldwide who reach the site from a plain-text line in the free Android app or from opt-in emails; Europe and the Arab world are the main markets, with some US.

## Product Purpose

Watabook is a car logbook that also explains what is wrong: offline fault-code dictionary, 36 dashboard warning-light meanings, service reminders, cost tracking and a document vault. This site explains the app, links to the stores, and (from November 2026) sells Watabook Pro to Android users through PayPal, because Google Play cannot pay merchants in Morocco.

## Positioning

No dongle, no account, no ads, works fully offline. A fault code is explained in plain words with an urgency verdict (safe to drive / gently to a garage / stop as soon as safe), next to the car's own logbook.

## Operating Context

- Free app on Google Play and the App Store. On iOS, Pro is sold with Apple in-app purchase. On Android, Pro is sold only on this website.
- Web Pro plans: Yearly 7.99 USD with a 7-day free trial, and Lifetime 19.99 USD (one payment). No monthly plan on the web (PayPal's fixed fee takes about 20% of a 1.99 payment).
- After payment, the buyer gets a license key by email (and on the page). They paste it in the app; a key works on up to 3 devices; old devices can be freed.
- Payments: PayPal (subscription for Yearly, one-time order for Lifetime). Licenses and emails are handled by a separate service at license.devandrepair.com (sandbox: license-service-sandbox.hassan-boulhilt.workers.dev).
- Refund policy: full refund within 14 days of payment, all plans; a refunded key stops working.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site on Cloudflare Pages, no build step, no framework. The repo is public: only public values (PayPal client ID, plan IDs) may appear in it.
- 7 languages: en and fr hand-written, ar/de/es/pt/it generated by tools/build_locales.py. Arabic is right-to-left.
- Store rules: the Android app may only show a plain-text mention of the website (no link, no button); the iOS app must never mention the website or web prices. The website itself may show prices and offers.
- Undecided: whether the home page's own pricing section moves to USD and links to /pro.

## Brand Commitments

- The site mirrors the app's signed-off "Service Manual" design system (watabook repo DESIGN.md): paper ground, real ink, one blueprint-blue accent, Spectral headings, IBM Plex Sans body, IBM Plex Mono for every number, code, price and date.
- App icon (since November 2026): yellow engine-check light on navy (#FBBF24 on #111827).
- Voice: plain, calm, honest. No hype, no ads, no dark patterns.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real app screenshots/mockup content on the home page (P0301 misfire example, logbook rows).
- No testimonials, ratings, user counts or press. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Honest and plain: say what you get, what it costs, and how to get your money back.
2. Offline-first and private: no account, no tracking claims beyond what is true.
3. One look across app and site: the site is the app's manual, not a separate brand.
4. Respect the stores' rules in the apps; do all selling on the web.

2. Description of Service

Watabook is a car-maintenance logbook. It stores your vehicles, service and fuel entries, reminders and documents locally on your device, and offers an offline reference dictionary of OBD-II fault codes and dashboard warning lights. An optional “Explain simply” feature sends a fault code to our AI processing service for a plain-language explanation (see §6 and §7).

Watabook is a reference and record-keeping tool. It is not a diagnostic instrument, does not connect to your vehicle, and does not replace inspection or repair by a qualified mechanic.

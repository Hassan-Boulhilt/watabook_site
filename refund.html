Refund Policy

Effective: 10 November 2026 Last updated: 10 November 2026 DevAndRepair

This policy covers Watabook Pro bought on this website with PayPal. If you bought Pro in the App Store or on Google Play, Apple or Google handle refunds under their own policies.

1. Full refund within 14 days

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of any payment, for any plan: the yearly plan (including a renewal payment) and the lifetime plan. You do not need to give a reason.

2. Free trial

The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial. If you cancel during the trial, nothing is charged, so there is nothing to refund. Cancel in your PayPal account: Settings, Payments, Automatic payments.

3. How to ask

Email hello@devandrepair.com from the address you used with PayPal, and include your licence key or the PayPal transaction ID. We refund through PayPal, to the same account or card, usually within 3 working days. PayPal decides how long the money takes to reach you.

4. What happens to your key

When a payment is refunded, the licence key it paid for stops working on all devices. The free features of Watabook keep working, and your data stays on your phone.

5. Cancelling the yearly plan

Cancelling stops future renewals. It does not refund the current year on its own; your key keeps working until the end of the year you paid for. If the last payment was less than 14 days ago, you can also ask for a refund as described above.

6. PayPal disputes

Please email us first; we answer refund requests quickly. If you open a dispute or chargeback with PayPal or your bank, the key is paused while it is open.

7. Your legal rights

This policy adds to your rights under the law of your country; it does not reduce them.

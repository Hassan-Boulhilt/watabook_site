§ 01–04What Watabook does
§ 01

Understand a warning light

36 dashboard symbols explained — what each one means and what to do. Type or photograph an OBD-II fault code for a plain-language answer, a “can I keep driving?” verdict, likely causes and a rough cost range. Works with zero signal.

§ 02

Never miss a service

Oil, filters, brake fluid, tyres, timing belt, insurance, technical inspection — remind by date, by mileage, or whichever comes first. Recurring reminders roll forward the moment you mark them done.

§ 03

One logbook per car

Every service, fill-up, expense and note — with the odometer, cost and photos. Fuel economy and cost-per-kilometre worked out for you, so you always know what your car really costs.

§ 04

Keep the paperwork

Registration, insurance and receipts, encrypted on your phone. Export everything as CSV any time — your data is never locked in.

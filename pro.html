Good to know

New phone?

Use the same key. If all three places are taken, free an old device from the app, or ask for a reset link by email.

Cancel the yearly plan

In PayPal: Settings, Payments, Automatic payments. Your key keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.

Refunds

Full refund within 14 days of a payment, for any plan. After a refund the key stops working. Refund policy.

Already bought on Google Play?

You keep Pro. The app still recognises your Google Play purchase; you do not need a key.

Lost your key?

Enter the email you used with PayPal. If we have a key for it, we send it again.
